# Election board second pass — October 27, 2026

Local implementation and verification report. This does not certify a production NBC feed or a deployed release. The NBC adapter, verification gates, control state machine and server are unchanged by this pass.

1. **Layout.** `/results` is a dedicated 100vw × 100dvh application. The normal header, footer, hero and content-width wrapper are absent from the markup. Office tabs and the compact chamber meter float over the map. Desktop race details overlay it; phone details use an expandable bottom sheet (46dvh normally, up to 75dvh expanded).

2. **Map-space measurement.** In the same browser at 1366 × 768, the old map rectangle started at y=492.47 and extended below the screen. Its visible canvas occupied about 335,881 pixels, or 32.0% of the viewport. The new canvas is 1,049,088 pixels, or 100%: approximately 3.12 times the visible canvas area (+212%). This measures the canvas before floating overlays, not the area of land polygons. D3 now sizes its projection/viewBox from the actual canvas, including on resize.

3. **Public cleanup.** Removed generic race context, roster warnings, mapping details, provider diagnostics, normal site navigation and implementation prose. The selected card prioritizes race, provenance-aware call, candidate/party, votes, vote percentage, real reporting data, margin, valid active shift and last update. Missing results display dashes. Actual NBC results can receive attribution; healthy status is quiet. An outage gets only “Results may be delayed” and a last-update age when known. The separate lab remains visibly simulated.

4. **Menu.** One small top-right, touch-sized button opens exactly Return to main site and the theme toggle. Keyboard activation, Escape dismissal and return of focus to the button were verified. Office tabs support left/right arrows. Both light and dark themes were checked.

5. **Toss-up sources and dates.** The editable snapshot uses [Cook House ratings](https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/house-race-ratings), dated October 23; [Cook Senate ratings](https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/senate-race-ratings), dated October 20; and [Cook governor ratings](https://www.cookpolitical.com/ratings/governor-race-ratings), dated October 23, 2026. Checked October 27. Direct pages returned 403; verification used publicly search-delivered versions of Cook's aggregate tables. This is an explicitly dated manual snapshot, not a live scraper or a guarantee against subsequent changes. Conflicting older snippets/detail-page templates were not used to replace the newer aggregate table.

6. **Watch management.** Edit `data/election-night-watchlist.json`, keyed by existing race ID. `tossup: true` represents the external rating; `watch: true` is FEA's independent editorial selection. There are 60 distinct listed races, 27 Toss-ups, and 50 FEA watch flags, with overlap. The previous 49 editorial selections were retained and CO-04 added. CO-08 remains To watch, not an externally labeled Toss-up. The compact Watch races selector and map outlines use this file. A missing file produces no invented labels. Unknown catalogue IDs are excluded. No JavaScript edit or new ratings editor is needed. Source-driven Toss-up corrections should update the source date/evidence too.

8. **Comparison bugs found and corrected.** House presidential comparison previously fell back to a statewide presidential margin. It now requires an explicitly geography-verified district row whose geometry ID matches the race, and never uses a state row. Both current and historical margins use `(D votes − R votes) / all candidate votes × 100`; shift is current minus baseline. Missing/invalid numbers do not become zero. The presidential builder also added TOTAL records to their component ballot modes: Arkansas 2024 D/R totals were doubled. It now uses TOTAL once, otherwise source partitions, and excludes bookkeeping rows. Alaska legislative reporting districts are excluded from county-map joins.

   The county aggregation alone did not exactly reconcile with official statewide totals. Available **2024 statewide** comparisons therefore use all 51 official [FEC state rows](https://www.fec.gov/resources/cms-content/documents/2024presgeresults.pdf), transcribed with page provenance in `data/baselines/source/fec-president-2024-states.json`. Controls match D 75,017,613, R 77,302,580 and total 155,238,302. County detail uses the local [MEDSL source](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/VOQCHQ), version 20260225, independently joined by Census FIPS; statewide totals are not reconstructed from these county rows. Differences are recorded in `pres-2024-states.json`. County coverage is 3,103 mapped units for 2024; unmatched units remain unavailable. This is not precinct-by-precinct source certification.

9. **District baseline status.** No verified 2024 presidential baseline on current 2026 House geometry was created. Existing cached `presidentialMargin2024` numbers lack the required current-map provenance. The repository's current-map configuration marks all 435 districts noncomparable, and the ledger has zero verified current-map anchors. The national geometry contains 436 features including the DC delegate, mixed historical metadata and a California merge; its “119” filename alone does not establish the 2026 crosswalk. The extra DC delegate is excluded from the 435-seat House race list. This audit does not assert that every district boundary is wrong; it establishes that the necessary matching evidence is missing.

10. **Unavailable comparisons.** Presidential shift is unavailable for **all 435 House districts**, including at-large districts. House shows Live results only. Senate and governor retain the verified 2024 statewide presidential option. The rebuilt 2020 data remains hidden pending official-total reconciliation. 2024 House shift also remains hidden pending district/source verification; last-Senate comparison needs seat-cycle/source validation; the previous governor file used synthetic vote counts derived from model margin inputs and remains hidden. Existing files are retained for audit, not represented as certified returns. Choices are filtered by office and valid race-level baseline, with no public diagnostic paragraphs.

11. **Admin simplification.** Main areas are Overview, Calls, Races, Broadcast and Testing, with Advanced last. Overview has four understandable metrics and items requiring attention. Calls show large race/candidate names and Publish, Hold and Override; lesser actions are disclosed. Races lead with candidates and totals, followed by expected vote, NBC, FEA and the operator choice. Analyst notes are collapsed inside Races. Saves and feed conditions use ordinary language. Candidate rows refresh without destroying an in-progress call form or closing Advanced details.

12. **Advanced.** URLs, fetch times, raw IDs/state, polling/health, audit evidence and backup diagnostics remain available in Advanced or race Advanced details. Normal state labels say Waiting to publish, Held for review and FEA override. The operator save warning says “Saved on this server. Backup sync failed.” A call-card bug found during browser QA is fixed: a manual override displays FEA's chosen candidate first and the conflicting NBC projection separately.

13. **Map interaction.** City labels have SVG/CSS pointer-events none, user-select none (including WebKit), aria-hidden true and focusable false. They do not intercept polygon interactions. Polygon selection, watch selection, zoom/reset, selected-state/district views and closing race details were exercised. Mobile keeps visual contact with the map; votes and percentages both remain visible in the three-column card.

Changed-script syntax and `git diff --check` pass. Browser QA covered 1366×768, 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 820×1180 and 390×844; full viewport canvas/viewBox, no document overflow, race selection, mobile expansion, menu/Escape/focus, theme, offices and comparison choices. Hold, publish, override and return-to-NBC were exercised in a temporary isolated copy; final conflicting-candidate override and return-to-NBC were rechecked after the fix. Test fixtures are synthetic and do not prove NBC general-election projection semantics. Production calls/provider settings were not changed.
