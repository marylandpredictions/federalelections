What these numbers mean

Polling error, not the state’s partisan lean

A +3 bias means polls overstated the Democratic D−R margin by three points. It does not mean the state voted Democratic, and it is not a prediction that the same error will repeat.

Historical evidence and a 2026 starting estimate

The historical table includes Senate contests from 2014 through 2024. The source provides a raw weighted state bias, a Census-division prior, evidence weights, a coherence multiplier and a final 2026 prior. These values are reproduced as supplied; no new weighting formula or forecast is introduced here.

Uneven evidence matters

The confidence labels are qualitative source labels, not probabilities or confidence intervals. Oklahoma has no usable standard D-vs-R modern Senate race in the source and uses its division prior. It is gray on the historical map, while its 2026 prior remains visible. Historical outliers retain both raw and capped bias values in the race table.
