House

Method.

How the House model turns district inputs, national signals, and simulation error into a control forecast.

Model built and maintained by gamerdoglover.

Inputs
Ratings
District ratings for the full 435-seat board, with public handicappers used as the main baseline.
Polling
Generic-ballot blend from the same public adapters used by the Senate model. District polling is blended only when stable district rows are available.
Districts
Open seats, presidential baseline, congressional baseline, rating bucket error, state-level correlation, national error, and current district status.
Map basis
Primary display
435-district cartogram with a shape-map fallback when the district GeoJSON loads.
Boundary source
Current 119th congressional district boundaries through the public district boundary service.
Redistricting
Handled through individual district inputs; no extra top-line party bonus is added after seats are modeled.
