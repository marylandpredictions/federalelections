Federal Elections Analysis is a political and electoral based group that works to ensure daily and evidence-driven analysis on federal, state, and local races. The group has been uploading electoral and political content on YouTube since 2024.

Our team is made up of a diverse group of individuals who enjoy election analysis. Our unified goal is to bring the data to you. We hope that you enjoy this website, our YouTube channel, and our community.

All of us share something in common with elections. It is not just the numbers and data, it is the people involved, the community, and the service. Republican or Democrat, we want to make that easier for you through our YouTube channel and this site.

You can support us by subscribing to our channel and liking our videos. To go a step further, you can buy a YouTube membership starting at just $1.99.
